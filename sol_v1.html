What is the computation?

1 · Dendrites are analog filtersEach track is a compartmental cable obeying a leak-and-diffusion update. Longer tracks spread and attenuate the same input. This is the part inspired by Operaattori.
2 · Soma combines arrivalsThree proximal voltages are integrated. A small explicit coincidence term stands for nonlinear dendritic integration. Two insufficient arrivals can jointly cross the gate.
3 · Turntable is an excitable phaseThe Adler-like phase sits at a stable equilibrium. Enough soma input pushes it past a separatrix; a complete 2π slip causes a standardized outgoing event.
4 · The spike carries an eventThe AIS event is converted to a separate short pulse at the receiving station. This is the transducer missing in the earlier chain relay tests—and it is deliberately added here, not claimed to emerge naturally. Toggle Relay ON/OFF to separate internal events from externally transmitted events.

Biology boundary: Neuronal dendrites do filter and integrate electrical input, and the AIS often initiates spikes. But real axons normally distribute an action potential through branches rather than using a rotating railway switch. Here route selection and phase-write are hypothetical computational mechanisms. No neuronal physiology or new physical law is being claimed.
